Biography

Short: Subchi is an open-format DJ and selector, and the creator of the Bentley Radio mix series — genre-blind sets built to keep any room moving. Long: Subchi (Sahib Singh) is an open-format DJ who moves effortlessly between hip-hop, house, afrobeats and bass within a single set. His ongoing Bentley Radio series — six episodes and counting — translates that live energy into recorded mixes, each a snapshot of his evolving taste. Equally at home in clubs, at festivals and on private floors, Subchi’s brief never changes: read the room, trust the record, keep people moving.
